                                                             Humans and other animals use powerful reinforcement learning (RL) mechanisms
Published online: 22 October 2025
                                                             that have been discovered by evolution over many generations of trial and error.
Open access
                                                             By contrast, artificial agents typically learn using handcrafted learning rules. Despite
    Check for updates                                        decades of interest, the goal of autonomously discovering powerful RL algorithms
                                                             has proven to be elusive1–6. Here we show that it is possible for machines to discover a
                                                             state-of-the-art RL rule that outperforms manually designed rules. This was achieved
                                                             by meta-learning from the cumulative experiences of a population of agents across
                                                             a large number of complex environments. Specifically, our method discovers the
                                                             RL rule by which the agent’s policy and predictions are updated. In our large-scale
                                                             experiments, the discovered rule surpassed all existing rules on the well-established
                                                             Atari benchmark and outperformed a number of state-of-the-art RL algorithms on
                                                             challenging benchmarks that it had not seen during discovery. Our findings suggest
                                                             that the RL algorithms required for advanced artificial intelligence may soon be
                                                             automatically discovered from the experiences of agents, rather than manually
                                                             designed.
